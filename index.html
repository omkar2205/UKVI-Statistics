Data Guide

Datasets used

Sponsored Applied is used for application volume. Sponsored study is used for outcome analysis.

Outcome scope

Only issued/granted and refused decisions are included. Withdrawn, lapsed and other outcomes are excluded.

Rates

Grant Rate = Issued / (Issued + Refused). Refusal Rate = Refused / (Issued + Refused).

Interpretation

Applications and outcomes are period-level indicators, not matched applicant-level records. Use applications vs outcomes directionally.
