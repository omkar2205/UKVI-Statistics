Data Guide

Applications are summed from Sponsored Applied. Outcomes are summed from Sponsored study, using only issued/granted and refused decisions.

Grant Rate = Issued / (Issued + Refused). Refusal Rate = Refused / (Issued + Refused).

Important: applications and outcomes are period-level indicators, not matched applicant-level records. An application submitted in one quarter may receive a decision in another quarter.
