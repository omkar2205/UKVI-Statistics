How to read this portal

Data definitions and interpretation notes

Applications

Applications are summed from the Sponsored Applied sheet.

Outcomes

Outcomes are summed from the Sponsored study sheet and include only issued/granted and refused decisions.

Rates

Grant Rate = Issued / (Issued + Refused). Refusal Rate = Refused / (Issued + Refused).

Important limitation

Applications and outcomes are period-level indicators, not matched applicant-level records. An application submitted in one quarter may receive a decision in another quarter.
